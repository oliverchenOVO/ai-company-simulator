## Compensation Model

Role-relative game-design anchor × relevant existing skill × bounded existing ambition preference; deterministic existing identity generation, no new random stream or Math.random. Integer cents with BigInt intermediate arithmetic and explicit rounding. Acceptance minimum uses stable risk tolerance,86.5–92.5% of expectation for generated ordinary candidates. Offer and expectation are distinct; expectation remains fixed after raises/cuts. Underpayment continues through existing bounded psychology equations; overpayment saturates at zero compensation dissatisfaction. No career/market/annual-review model added.

Initial founders retain explicit scenario contractual commitments25k/40k/30k; these are predetermined founding arrangements, not player recruiting offers. New recruits receive independently derived expectation. Recruitment UI exposes relevant skill, expectation, minimum, actual offer, payroll/runway preview and normal rejection explanation; previous rejected offer is distinguished from the next candidate. Whole-NT$ guidance rounds minimum upward while simulation uses exact cents. Hidden unrelated psychology remains private.
