# Phase1.5 story and causal audit

These chains use saved events, real commands and structured cause classifications. They are not fictional scenes. Causal links, time ordering and controlled effects are distinguished; an eventId reference is not proof of a unique cause.

## Financial stories — benchmark-001

Passive: feature development launches Atlas atday64; small contracts begin. Initial95k payroll +10k operating expense outpaces receipts. Under3-month warning atday59, forecast unsafe/under1-month atday151, actual insolvency day181:122 days after the severe warning and30 days after the unsafe next-close indication. Conservative: founder pay10k, sustainable workload, timely payroll downsizing and quality focus → lower burn, then contracts catch up. The same-seed financial branch atday90 survives today455 with NT$605,547 vs control insolvency day181 and NT$−39,834. Recovery is caused by a bundled intervention; its constituent individual effects are measured separately, not attributed solely to quality.

Aggressive: hires earlier and launches faster; under3-month signal day15, under1-month/unsafe close day59, insolvency day90. Early signals leave75 and31 days respectively. Monthly earned liabilities explain why stopping recruitment later cannot erase past payroll. No “mathematically inevitable” date is asserted: legal payroll changes, firing and uncertain future contracts can still alter the future. Next-close insufficiency is a conditional financial projection, not proof of inevitability.

## Customer uncertainty

The late growth trace records37 churn events, all classified customer-budget/external; no product-experience churn or prior followup status was observed in this sample. This is a genuine modeled random weekly churn opportunity, not a simulation of a known budget shock. Do not invent customer complaints, a forecast or warning that did not exist. UI now states the recorded classification and its limited evidence. Product satisfaction declines can produce the separate product-experience classification, but these sessions did not validate that path. This is a remaining coverage/product-causality limitation, not hidden success.

## Event noise and presentation

PayrollProcessed and FinancialClose form repetitive monthly pairs, while CustomerAcquired/Churned can dominate a long-lived timeline. The raw benchmark records per-type counts, major-event counts and longest spacing; stopped years are not counted as meaningful quiet gameplay. UI retains ALL authoritative events. Inbox defaults to important/warning first with explicit historical-date warning and an off switch; timeline stays chronological and can filter critical/warning/important/informational. Employee-related causes and prior observations link without fabricating causal edges. Weekly summaries list up to4 prioritized actual new events, money/MRR/product/people/customer differences and current alerts. Daily steps do not interrupt. No new narrative author or artificial drama.
