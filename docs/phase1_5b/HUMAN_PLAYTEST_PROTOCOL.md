# Human playtest protocol

Status: prepared; no external human participants available in this run. Automated policies and Codex-operated Playwright are engineering QA, not human research.

Recruit3–5 independent participants with consent, record anonymous P01–P05 identifiers only. Do not collect names, contact details or sensitive personal information. Each uses an isolated browser profile, new Garage Startup, one assigned shared seed; do not give optimal salary/cost strategies. Allow12 simulated months or bankruptcy, approximately30–45 minutes. Observer records actual commands/dates, first warning noticed, payroll understanding, rejected offer reaction, recovery attempts and final outcome. Avoid coaching unless necessary to continue; label assistance.

Before session: explain local browser progress, export availability and that failure is possible. Ask participant to think aloud. Record time to identify urgent issue on starting overview and first crisis, rather than inferring it from automation speed. At first hiring attempt ask what they expect the monthly cost and runway effect to be, then compare with actual acceptance/payroll. Observe whether the candidate expectation, minimum acceptance and actual offer are distinct in their explanation.

After session ask:

- What caused your largest financial problem?
- Did you understand expected hiring cost?
- Did any outcome feel unfair or random?
- When did you first realize the company was in danger?
- What decision would you change?
- Did hiring feel useful?
- Did salary decisions make sense?
- Could you explain why an employee or customer changed state?

Record evidence against actual exported commands/events, do not substitute observer speculation. Note confusion, warning lead time as noticed by participant, salary expectation misconceptions, and whether recruitment rejection felt like a normal candidate response. Allow participant to remove their anonymous session evidence. Aggregate small sample qualitatively; do not claim statistical generalizability. A future results file must include actual participant count and any unavailable questions.
