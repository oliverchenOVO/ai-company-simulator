# Phase 2 human playtest protocol

Actual external human participants: **0**. No human feedback or attachment is fabricated. Automated strategy policies and Codex-controlled browser/Electron actions are separate evidence and cannot establish emotional engagement.

Protocol: recruit 3–5 participants for an independent browser profile using a fixed seed. Keep initial cash/rules unchanged. Observe 30–45 minutes, first create, inspect employee career/manager, progress through a concern, reorganize/promote, inspect Why, export and refresh. Save consented feedback outside the repository; never commit personal saves or identifying information. Use separate export checkpoints for facilitator analysis after play, not hidden numeric explanations during play.

Ask: why was this person unhappy; how did managers differ; what did team movement cost; did promotion have a tradeoff; could overload be identified; which person mattered; did any departure feel arbitrary; how did career action differ from salary; why did output change? Record task success, pauses/confusions and quoted consented feedback separately from simulation metrics. Do not report a human pass until people actually participate.

Current product limitation: the functional reporting table favors clear relations over a spatial tree. Testing whether players remember people and interpret causal chains remains the next human acceptance exercise.
