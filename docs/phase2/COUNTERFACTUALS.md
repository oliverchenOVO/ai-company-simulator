All branches start from an identical validated state and run 180 days. Capitalized origin (NT$100m) isolates organization from early bankruptcy; this is not the normal Garage economy. The hiring-overload branch changes roster/pay, so its cash difference is not a pure management benefit. The matched-roster capacity branch keeps roster, pay and the focused employee’s manager identical, redistributing other reports to deputies. It improves focused manager trust by approximately 9 points, but weak deputies reduce team coordination and total work: management decentralization is not universally beneficial.

Salary-only and specialist promotion differ: career-3 promotion reduces frustration and exit intent, whereas benchmark-010 already pursues mastery and promotion creates more unmet compensation expectation. Stress in these sustainable 180-day branches reaches its lower bound, so this table does not claim a universal stress improvement. Actual resignation counts remain zero in these protected branches; exit-intent differences are risk evidence, not predicted guaranteed departures.
